Stability Index

Statistical scoring of local stability to guide stabilization programming. I compute stability scores with logistic regression, maintain the regional database and compile the cross-country reports.

Climate Vulnerability Assessment — Mauritania

Key-informant survey across 196 localities in Hodh Ech Chargui and Guidimakha. I designed the instrument, ran the machine-learning analysis — random forests, gradient boosting, logistic regression — producing a mobility typology (mobile, mixed, trapped, stable), and authored the report and policy deck.
